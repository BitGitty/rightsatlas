မန်းတောင်ရိပ်ခို

"Man Taung Yeik Kho" by Myoma Nyein (မြို့မငြိမ်း) is one of the classic Burmese songs we have checked. One of the best-loved Thingyan (Burmese New Year) songs. (source)

In Myanmar

Public domain in Myanmar since 1 January 2006. The composer, Myoma Nyein, died in 1955. Myanmar protects songs for the author's life plus 50 years, counted from 1 January after the death, so the song's Myanmar copyright ended on 31 December 2005. (composer, law)

In the US

The song was written for that year's Thingyan festival. For the US: Myanmar works still protected in Myanmar on 1 January 1996 had their US copyright restored for 95 years from first publication; if this song was first published in 1947, it is protected in the US until 31 December 2042. We have not confirmed the year of first publication, so we do not give a US verdict yet. (US Copyright Office) Read why in our explainer: Burmese songs, Myanmar vs US copyright.
