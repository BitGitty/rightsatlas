Is "My Blue Heaven" public domain? Yes. The song has been free in the US since 1 January 2023 at the latest. Anyone can now perform it, record a new version, or print the sheet music without permission.

The evidence

The music is by Walter Donaldson and the lyrics are by George A. Whiting. Wikipedia's infobox lists the song as published on 10 October 1927 by Leo Feist Inc. The UW-Madison Mills Music Library's sheet music catalog dates the same published sheet music to 1927 and credits the same two writers, so two sources agree on the year. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1927, that is 2023.

Recordings are a different right

A free song is not a free recording. See our explainer on why a song and a recording are different copyrights: a sound recording runs on its own, later clock. Gene Austin's original 1927 recording, which sold over five million copies, is a protected recording on its own schedule, separate from the song.

Where people know it from

Gene Austin's 1927 recording sold more than five million copies and was inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame in 1978 (source).
