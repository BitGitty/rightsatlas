"Heartaches" took sixteen years to become a hit. It took ninety-six to become free. Here is what we found about its US copyright.

The short answer

The song is public domain in the US. It was written and published in 1931, so it became free on 1 January 2027 at the latest, and that date has now arrived.

The evidence

The music is by Al Hoffman and the lyrics are by John Klenner. Wikipedia states it was written and published in 1931. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1931, that is 2027.

Recordings are a different right

A free song does not make every recording free. A sound recording has its own copyright, with its own clock. The song sat quietly for sixteen years before Ted Weems and his Orchestra took it to number one in 1947; that 1947 recording is a separate, later copyright, still running on its own schedule even though the 1931 song itself is now free.

Where people know it from

Ted Weems and his Orchestra's 1947 recording took the song to number one, sixteen years after it was first published (source).
