Eleven days into 2027, and the "class of 1931" we listed in our New Year roundup is already free. "Life Is Just a Bowl of Cherries" was one of the eighteen songs on that list; here is a deeper look at this one.

The short answer

"Life Is Just a Bowl of Cherries" is now public domain in the US. It was published in 1931, so it became free on 1 January 2027 at the latest. Anyone can now record it, perform it, or print the sheet music without permission.

The evidence

The music is by Ray Henderson, with lyrics by Lew Brown. Wikipedia states it was published in 1931 by DeSylva, Brown & Henderson, Inc. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest; for 1931 that is 2027.

Recordings are a different right

The song's own copyright has run out, but any specific recording of it is a separate, protected copyright. A sound recording has its own clock, regardless of how old the underlying song is. If you need the tune for a video, the safe path is to perform or record it yourself.

Where people know it from

Ethel Merman introduced the phrase, and the song, in George White's Scandals of 1931 (source), where it quickly became a catchphrase of the Depression era.
