Is Charade public domain? The picture is, and it has been since the day it opened in 1963. The story behind that is one of the clearest copyright-notice mistakes in film history, and the music never went with it.

What makes Charade public domain?

Universal's own release prints of Charade carried a defective copyright notice, missing elements the 1909 Copyright Act required. Under that law, a defective notice on publication put the work straight into the US public domain, no renewal needed, because there was never a valid copyright to renew. It is one of the most-cited notice-failure cases in film-copyright literature, and the film has circulated openly on the Internet Archive for two decades without a challenge to the picture itself.

Is the music in Charade also free?

  • Score: still protected. Henry Mancini's score, including the title song "Charade" with lyrics by Johnny Mercer, was registered and renewed separately as a musical work. The music is still under copyright even though the picture is free, which is why "public domain" uploads of Charade still draw legitimate audio claims.
  • Story: not verified. The film is based on "The Unsuspecting Wife," a 1961 Redbook story by Peter Stone and Marc Behm. We have no renewal search on file for that story, and the Supreme Court's Stewart v. Abend ruling means a still-protected underlying story can be infringed by exploiting the film, so we are not calling this layer clear.
  • Trademark: likely public domain. No active marks on the title or characters are known to us.
  • Restorations: partially protected. A faithful restoration of a public-domain film adds no new US copyright, but the Criterion Collection and Universal HD masters are modern transfers that may carry their own additions. Use an archival copy instead.

Who made Charade?

Stanley Donen directed Charade, a romantic mystery starring Cary Grant and Audrey Hepburn, released on 5 December 1963. It was shot on location in Paris, with interior work at the Studios de Boulogne. Grant, then 59, was uneasy about the 25-year age gap with 33-year-old Hepburn, so the filmmakers added dialogue about his age and let Hepburn's character do the pursuing.
