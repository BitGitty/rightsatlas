ပန်းဟေဝန်

"Pan Hay Won" by Shwe Pyi Aye (ရွှေပြည်အေး) is one of the classic Burmese songs we have checked. Still popular in Myanmar today. (source)

In Myanmar

Protected in Myanmar until 31 December 2027. The composer, Shwe Pyi Aye, died in 1977. Myanmar protects songs for the author's life plus 50 years, counted from 1 January after the death, so the song's Myanmar copyright lasts until 31 December 2027. (composer, law)

In the US

The song was recorded in July 1935 with Ma Aye Mi. For the US: a Myanmar work got its US copyright back on 1 January 1996 (the URAA) only if ALL of these hold: it was still protected in Myanmar on that date, at least one author was a Myanmar national or resident, it was first published outside the US, and it was not published in the US within 30 days. If they all hold and the song was first published in 1935, it is protected in the US until 31 December 2030. We have not confirmed the year of first publication, so we do not give a US verdict yet.
