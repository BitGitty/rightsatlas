Is "Santa Claus Is Comin' to Town" public domain? Not yet. The 1934 Christmas standard is still under US copyright, and it becomes free on 1 January 2030 at the latest.

The short answer

The music is by J. Fred Coots and the lyrics are by Haven Gillespie. Wikipedia states the song was written by Coots and Gillespie and published in 1934 by Leo Feist, Inc. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1934, that is 2030.

Recordings are a different right

Even after the song's own term ends, recordings of it will stay on their own separate clocks. See our explainer on why a song and a recording are different copyrights. The Crystals' 1963 recording, produced by Phil Spector, is a protected sound recording with its own copyright, entirely apart from the 1934 composition.

Where people know it from

The Crystals' 1963 recording, produced by Phil Spector for "A Christmas Gift for You from Phil Spector," made the song a holiday radio staple. Its refrain was later covered by the Jackson 5, Bruce Springsteen and Mariah Carey (source).
