Is "Goodnight, Sweetheart" public domain? Yes, as of 1 January 2027. The song just finished its 95-year US copyright term, along with the rest of the class of 1931.

The evidence

The song was written by three people together, Ray Noble, Jimmy Campbell and Reg Connelly, and published by Campbell Connelly & Co. in London in 1931, according to Wikipedia. It was first published in the UK, not the US, but foreign works follow the same 95-year US term, counted from their original publication date abroad. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1931, that is 2027, so the song is now free since 1 January 2027 at the latest, right alongside the rest of the US class of 1931.

Recordings are a different right

Being free as a song does not make any recording of it free. See our explainer on why a song and a recording are different copyrights. Al Bowlly's hit recording with the Ray Noble Orchestra is a separate, protected sound recording, apart from the composition, and so is any newer cover version recorded since.
