Is "Just One More Chance" public domain? Yes, as of 1 January 2027. The song just finished its 95-year US copyright term, along with the rest of the class of 1931.

The evidence

The music is by Arthur Johnston and the lyrics are by Sam Coslow. The song was published by Famous Music Corp. in New York in 1931, according to Wikipedia's page on Johnston. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1931, that is 2027, so the song is now free since 1 January 2027 at the latest, right alongside the rest of the US class of 1931.

Recordings are a different right

A free composition does not free any one recording of it. See our explainer on why a song and a recording are different copyrights. Bing Crosby's original recording stays a separate, protected sound recording, apart from the composition itself. That holds no matter how old the recording is: age alone does not free a sound recording before its own, separate term runs out.

Where people know it from

Bing Crosby introduced the song in the 1931 film "One More Chance" (source), early in his career before he became one of the best-selling recording artists of the era.
