Is the song "Body and Soul" public domain? Yes, since 2026 The 1930 jazz standard "Body and Soul," by Johnny Green, has been free in the US since 1 January 2026 at the latest. There is a 1930 song called "Body and Soul," and a separate 1925 film by the same name that we have covered too. This post is about the song. Is it public domain? Yes.

Can you use "Body and Soul" without permission?

The song is public domain in the US. It was published in 1930, so it became free on 1 January 2026 at the latest. Anyone can now perform it, record it or print the sheet music without permission.

What's the evidence "Body and Soul" is public domain?

The music is by Johnny Green, and the lyrics are by Edward Heyman, Robert Sour and Frank Eyton. Wikipedia states it was published in 1930 by Harms. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1930, that is 2026.

Why isn't every version of "Body and Soul" free?

A free song does not make every recording free. A sound recording has its own copyright, with its own clock. Coleman Hawkins's famous 1939 recording is a separate, still-protected work, even though the song itself is not.

Where does "Body and Soul" show up in pop culture?

Coleman Hawkins's 11 October 1939 recording is one of the most celebrated jazz solos on record, and it was added to the Library of Congress National Recording Registry in 2004 (source).
