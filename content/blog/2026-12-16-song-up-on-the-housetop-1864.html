Is "Up on the Housetop" public domain? Yes. The song is more than 160 years old, and it has been free in the US for well over a century. Here is the evidence.

The evidence

Benjamin Hanby wrote the song in 1864, and it was first published in the magazine "Our Song Birds" by Root & Cady, according to Wikipedia. In the US, every work published in 1930 or earlier is public domain by age. A song from 1864 cleared that bar generations ago, so it has been free since 1 January 1960 at the latest, and in practice much earlier. Choirs, school pageants and recording artists have been free to use the words and melody however they like for decades, no permission needed.

Recordings are a different right

Being this old does not make every performance of it free. See our explainer on why a song and a recording are different copyrights: a sound recording carries its own, separate copyright with its own clock. Gene Autry's well-known 1953 recording is a protected recording in its own right, on a schedule that has nothing to do with Hanby's 1864 composition.

Where people know it from

Gene Autry's 1953 recording is one of the best-known versions of the carol (source).
