"Sonny Boy" was one of the biggest hits of the early talkie era. Is it public domain? Yes. Here is what we found about its US copyright.

Is the song "Sonny Boy" in the public domain?

The song is public domain in the US. It was published in 1928, so it became free on 1 January 2024 at the latest. Anyone can now perform it, record it or print the sheet music without permission.

When was "Sonny Boy" published?

The music is by Ray Henderson, and the lyrics are by B. G. DeSylva and Lew Brown. Wikipedia states it was composed and published in 1928 for the part-talkie film "The Singing Fool," introduced on screen by Al Jolson. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1928, that is 2024.

Can you use a recording of "Sonny Boy"?

A free song does not mean every recording of it is free. A sound recording has its own copyright, with its own clock. Al Jolson's own 1928 recording is a separate, still-protected work, even though the song itself is not.

"Sonny Boy": where do people know it from?

Al Jolson's 1928 recording was number one for twelve weeks and sold over a million copies (source).
