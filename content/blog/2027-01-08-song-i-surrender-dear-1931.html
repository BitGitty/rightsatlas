Is the song "I Surrender, Dear" in the public domain?

The song is now public domain in the US. It was published in 1931, so it became free on 1 January 2027. Anyone can now perform it, record a new version, or print the sheet music without permission.

When was "I Surrender, Dear" published?

Wikipedia states the song, with music by Harry Barris and lyrics by Gordon Clifford, was recorded on 19 January 1931 and published that year. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1931, that is 2027.

Can you use a recording of "I Surrender, Dear"?

The song being free does not make every recording free. A recording has its own, separate copyright on its own clock. The famous 1931 recording below stays protected even though the song itself is now free.

Where does "I Surrender, Dear" show up in pop culture?

It is best known from Bing Crosby's 1931 recording with Gus Arnheim's orchestra (source).
