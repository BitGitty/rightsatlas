Can you use "When It's Sleepy Time Down South" without permission?

The song is now public domain in the US. It was published in 1931, so it became free on 1 January 2027. Anyone can now perform it, record a new version, or print the sheet music without permission.

Who wrote "When It's Sleepy Time Down South"?

Wikipedia states the song, written by Clarence Muse, Leon Rene and Otis Rene, was published by Freed & Powers in Hollywood, with its copyright registered in 1931. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1931, that is 2027.

Why isn't every version of "When It's Sleepy Time Down South" free?

The song being free does not make every recording free. A recording has its own, separate copyright on its own clock. The famous recording below stays protected even though the song itself is now free.
