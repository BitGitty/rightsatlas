Is The Patchwork Girl of Oz public domain? Yes, the 1914 film print is free in the US. This is the silent film adaptation of L. Frank Baum's own 1913 novel, made by his own studio. The Oz name itself is a different question, and we have not answered it here.

What makes The Patchwork Girl of Oz public domain?

The film was published in 1914. Every US work published in 1930 or earlier is public domain by age, with no renewal check required, so the print is verified public domain. At 1914, this film is among the oldest surviving American feature films still watchable today.

Who made The Patchwork Girl of Oz?

L. Frank Baum wrote and produced the film for his own Oz Film Manufacturing Company, adapting his 1913 novel, with J. Farrell MacDonald directing. It was released on 28 September 1914 and was a commercial failure that caused distribution problems for the other Oz Film titles that followed. The title role went to acrobat Pierre Couderc because Baum could not find a woman with the needed acrobatic skill, and the film is one of only three surviving productions from the company, out of dozens it attempted to make (Wikipedia).
