Is Broken Blossoms public domain? Yes. D. W. Griffith's 1919 drama, and the short story it is based on, are both free to use in the US.

Is Broken Blossoms public domain?

Broken Blossoms premiered in New York on 13 May 1919 and went into wider US release that October. Works published before 1923 carried a flat maximum US copyright term of 75 years, so the film's term ran out no later than 1 January 1995, with no renewal search needed.

How was Broken Blossoms made?

D. W. Griffith directed and produced Broken Blossoms, distributed by United Artists, starring Lillian Gish as Lucy Burrows and Richard Barthelmess as Cheng Huan, with Donald Crisp as her father. It premiered at George M. Cohan's Theatre in New York on 13 May 1919 and was shot entirely in controlled studio settings to give it a deliberately "impressionistic" look, unlike Griffith's earlier large-scale films. It was the first film United Artists ever distributed, bought from Famous Players-Lasky for $250,000.
