Decades before Ella Fitzgerald and Noel Coward each made it their own, "Let's Do It, Let's Fall in Love" began its life on a Paris stage, written and performed entirely in French and English by Cole Porter.

Is "Let's Do It, Let's Fall in Love" free to use?

"Let's Do It, Let's Fall in Love" is public domain in the US. It was published in 1928, so the song became free on 1 January 2024 at the latest. Anyone can now record it, perform it, or print the sheet music without permission.

Who wrote "Let's Do It, Let's Fall in Love"?

Cole Porter wrote both the music and the lyrics. Wikipedia states the song was published in 1928 and introduced that year in Porter's Broadway musical Paris, performed by Irene Bordoni. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest; for 1928 that is 2024.

Can you use a recording of "Let's Do It, Let's Fall in Love"?

The song's copyright has run out, but a specific recording of it has its own, separate copyright. Bing Crosby's 1928 recording and every later cover are protected sound recordings in their own right, even though the song itself is not. If you need the tune for a video, the safe path is to perform or record it yourself.

Where do people know "Let's Do It, Let's Fall in Love" from?

Bing Crosby recorded it in 1928 with the Dorsey Brothers Orchestra, one of the song's earliest hit versions (source).
