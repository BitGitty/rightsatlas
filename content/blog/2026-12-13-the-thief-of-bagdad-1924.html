Is The Thief of Bagdad (1924) public domain? Yes. Douglas Fairbanks's silent fantasy adventure is free in the US, and so is the story it tells. The one thing to watch is the soundtrack on whatever copy you pick up.

Is The Thief of Bagdad public domain?

The film was published in 1924. The US copyright term for pre-1978 works tops out at 95 years, and every film published in 1930 or earlier has now run out that term, placing it in the public domain by age.

What other rights apply to The Thief of Bagdad?

The story is free too, for two reasons. It is freely adapted from One Thousand and One Nights, an ancient folk-tale collection with no surviving copyright, and the film's own screen story was published in 1924, so its own term has also expired. Trademark status for the title was not checked and stays open.

The music is where care is needed. The Internet Archive copy of the film carries an organ score by Gaylord Carter, and a reviewer on that page says the soundtrack is a recent addition still under copyright; we have not confirmed that ourselves, so treat the music as unverified rather than free. The same listing notes that 1980s reissues of the film added footage, interviews and new music that were copyrighted at the time — another reason to check exactly which edition you have before reusing it. We have not confirmed any specific restoration either, so that layer stays open too. The safest approach for a reuser is the same one that applies to most silent films on this site: take a clean transfer of the 1924 images, strip any audio that comes with it, and add music you actually control.

How was The Thief of Bagdad made?

Directed by Raoul Walsh and produced by and starring Fairbanks, the film is known for its enormous studio sets and elaborate special effects, including a flying carpet and a magic rope. Made on a budget then considered enormous for Hollywood, it is often called Fairbanks's personal masterpiece, and it was added to the National Film Registry in 1996 for its lasting influence on the fantasy-adventure genre.
