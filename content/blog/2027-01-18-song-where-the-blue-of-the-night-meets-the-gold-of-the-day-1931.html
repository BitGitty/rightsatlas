Is the song "Where the Blue of the Night" in the public domain?

The song is public domain in the US. It was published in 1931, so it became free on 1 January 2027 at the latest, and that date has now arrived.

When was "Where the Blue of the Night" published?

Wikipedia states the song was published and copyrighted in 1931, written by Roy Turk and Fred E. Ahlert with Bing Crosby himself, who recorded it, credited as a co-writer. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1931, that is 2027.

Can you use a recording of "Where the Blue of the Night"?

A free song does not make every recording free. A sound recording has its own copyright, with its own clock. Crosby's own recordings of his theme song are separate, still-protected works, even though the 1931 composition underneath them is now free.

"Where the Blue of the Night": where do people know it from?

It served as Bing Crosby's own radio theme song for years, opening his broadcasts for a generation of listeners (source). That makes it unusual on our 1931 list: most of the songs we track were written for a stage show or a film, but this one was written for radio, and for one specific performer.
