Is Way Down East public domain? Yes. D.W. Griffith's 1920 melodrama, about a young woman tricked into a sham marriage who is cast out into a blizzard, is free to use in the US. It is best remembered today for its climactic ice-floe rescue.

Is Way Down East public domain?

Way Down East was published in 1920. Every US work published in 1930 or earlier is public domain today by age, with no renewal search needed, so the print is verified public domain. That bright line is the simplest check on this site: a US film this old clears copyright on age alone, no matter how commercially successful it was at the time.

How was Way Down East made?

Griffith paid $175,000 for the rights to Lottie Blair Parker's stage play and turned it into a 148-minute melodrama distributed by United Artists. Released on 3 September 1920, it earned more than $4.5 million, one of his most successful films. Its famous ice-floe rescue was filmed in White River Junction, Vermont, with no stunt doubles. Lillian Gish performed the scene herself and suffered frostbite and lasting damage to her hand, trailing one hand in the icy water for the camera while drifting toward a waterfall on a real ice floe (Wikipedia).
