နှစ်သစ်ချစ်ဦး

"New Year Chit Oo" by Bogalay Tint Aung (ဘိုကလေးတင့်အောင်) is one of the classic Burmese songs we have checked. One of the composer's early popular songs. (source)

In Myanmar

Protected in Myanmar until 31 December 2071. The composer, Bogalay Tint Aung, died in 2021. Myanmar protects songs for the author's life plus 50 years, counted from 1 January after the death, so the song's Myanmar copyright lasts until 31 December 2071. (composer, law)

In the US

The song was written. For the US: a Myanmar work got its US copyright back on 1 January 1996 (the URAA) only if ALL of these hold: it was still protected in Myanmar on that date, at least one author was a Myanmar national or resident, it was first published outside the US, and it was not published in the US within 30 days. If they all hold and the song was first published in 1951, it is protected in the US until 31 December 2046. We have not confirmed the year of first publication, so we do not give a US verdict yet.
