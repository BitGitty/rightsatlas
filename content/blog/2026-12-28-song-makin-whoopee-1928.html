Is "Makin' Whoopee" public domain? Yes. The 1928 song has been free in the US since 1 January 2024 at the latest. Anyone can now perform it, arrange it, or print the sheet music without permission.

The evidence

The music is by Walter Donaldson and the lyrics are by Gus Kahn. Wikipedia states the song was published in 1928 by Donaldson, Douglas & Gumble, and popularized by Eddie Cantor in the Broadway musical "Whoopee!" that same year. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1928, that is 2024, so performers and publishers no longer need permission to use the song itself.

Recordings are a different right

A free song does not free every recording of it. See our explainer on why a song and a recording are different copyrights. Bing Crosby's recording with the Paul Whiteman Orchestra, made on 22 December 1928, is a protected sound recording with its own separate clock, apart from the song itself.

Where people know it from

Bing Crosby's recording with the Paul Whiteman Orchestra reached number 8 on the Billboard charts (source), and Eddie Cantor introduced the song on Broadway the same year it was published.
