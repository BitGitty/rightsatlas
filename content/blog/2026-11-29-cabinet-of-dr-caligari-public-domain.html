Is The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari public domain? Yes. The German horror film that invented the tilted sets and painted shadows of Expressionist cinema, and gave cinema one of its first great twist endings, is free to use in the US, a century on.

Is The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari public domain?

The film was published in 1920. Its full US copyright term expired long ago, and expiry like this cannot be reversed by the URAA, the law that restored some foreign works in 1996. So the print is public domain in the US regardless of its German origin, as the Cornell copyright term chart confirms for works this old.

What other rights apply to The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari?

  • Score: partially protected. The film was silent. Any music you hear on a modern copy was recorded later and belongs to whoever made it.
  • Story: public domain. The original screenplay, by Hans Janowitz and Carl Mayer, is also an expired 1920 work.
  • Trademark: likely free. We found no active marks tied to the film, though this was not a full search.
  • Restorations: partially protected. Copying or restoring public-domain images without new creative additions is not itself protected, under the reasoning in Bridgeman Art Library v. Corel Corp. So a faithful restoration, such as the Murnau Foundation's 2014 4K version, adds no new copyright to the images themselves, but a newly composed score, colorized frames, or newly written titles added along the way can be. Ripping a copy-protected disc is also a separate legal risk under 17 U.S.C. 1201, even when the underlying film is free.

How was The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari made?

Robert Wiene directed the film for the German studio Decla-Film. The story follows a hypnotist who uses a sleepwalker to commit murders in a nightmarish, distorted town. The writers, Hans Janowitz and Carl Mayer, were two pacifists shaped by their experience of the First World War, and the designers Hermann Warm, Walter Reimann and Walter Röhrig built the deliberately crooked, painted sets that launched German Expressionist cinema, according to Wikipedia.
