Is Africa Screams public domain? The evidence points that way, but our renewal search is not complete, so read this one as strong rather than certain.

Why the film print appears free

Africa Screams was released in 1949, putting its 28th-year renewal deadline in 1977. We scanned the 1976 and 1977 Catalog of Copyright Entries motion-picture volumes and found no renewal for "Africa Screams" in either one. That scan only covered the A-to-CBS portion of the 1976 index and the A-to-CHI portion of the 1977 index — large volumes, only partly read — and we have not yet run a Copyright Office public-catalog search either. So this is a real but unfinished result.

The other layers

The score, the story, and any trademark are all not verified. The film is a sound picture, so its recorded track would normally share the print's status, but we have not checked the music credits or whether any cue was separately published or renewed. The story is credited to Earl Baldwin, with a screenplay by Martin Ragaway and Leonard B. Stern, and we have not checked whether it adapts any earlier published work. Trademark in the title was likewise not checked.

The restoration history is also open. Film preservationist Bob Furmanek reportedly obtained the original nitrate and transferred it to 35mm for preservation in the late 1980s, and a Kickstarter-funded Blu-ray followed in June 2020. We have not confirmed what that 2020 release adds — new titles, commentary or other extras could carry their own separate rights even if the underlying footage does not. Until the renewal search above is finished and that restoration is checked, the safer sourcing choice is an older, archive-hosted copy rather than the 2020 disc.

A little history

Directed by Charles Barton, the film follows two department-store clerks, played by Bud Abbott and Lou Costello, who join a safari in search of diamonds and a legendary giant ape. Clyde Beatty and Shemp Howard also appear.
