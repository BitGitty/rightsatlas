Most people know this song from Marilyn Monroe, not from a 1928 Broadway musical. Is it public domain? Yes. Here is what we found.

Is "I Wanna Be Loved by You" public domain?

The song is public domain in the US. It was published in 1928, so it became free on 1 January 2024 at the latest. Anyone can perform it, record it or print the sheet music without permission.

How do we know "I Wanna Be Loved by You" is free?

The music is by Herbert Stothart and Harry Ruby, and the lyrics are by Bert Kalmar. Wikipedia states it was written and published in 1928 for the Broadway musical "Good Boy," which first opened on 5 September 1928. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1928, that is 2024.

Is every recording of "I Wanna Be Loved by You" free too?

A free song does not make every recording free. A sound recording has its own copyright, with its own clock. Marilyn Monroe's famous film performance is a separate, still-protected recording, even though the underlying song is not.

Where do people know "I Wanna Be Loved by You" from?

Marilyn Monroe's performance in the film "Some Like It Hot" (1959) is the version most people know today (source).
