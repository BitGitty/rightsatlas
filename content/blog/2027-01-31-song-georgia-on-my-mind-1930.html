Ray Charles made "Georgia on My Mind" a number-one hit in 1960, but the song is thirty years older than that recording. It has now been free of copyright for over a year.

Is the song "Georgia on My Mind" in the public domain?

"Georgia on My Mind" is public domain in the US. It was published in December 1930, so the song became free on 1 January 2026 at the latest. Anyone can now record it, perform it, or print the sheet music without permission.

When was "Georgia on My Mind" published?

The music is by Hoagy Carmichael, with lyrics by Stuart Gorrell. Wikipedia states it was written in 1930 and published on 22 December 1930 by Southern Music Publishing Co., Inc. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest; for 1930 that is 2026.

Can you use a recording of "Georgia on My Mind"?

The song's own copyright has run out, but any specific recording of it is a separate, protected copyright. Ray Charles's 1960 recording stays protected as a sound recording in its own right, even though the song underneath it is now free. If you need the tune for a video, the safe path is to perform or record it yourself.

Where might you have heard "Georgia on My Mind"?

Ray Charles's 1960 recording reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100, and the song was named Georgia's official state song in 1979 (source).
