Happy Public Domain Day. "Dancing in the Dark" is one of eighteen 1931 songs that just finished their 95-year US copyright term. We listed the whole class in our 1931 songs roundup; here is a closer look at this one.

The short answer

The song is now public domain in the US. It was published in 1931, so it became free on 1 January 2027. Anyone can now perform it, record a new version, or print the sheet music without permission. Note this is the 1931 standard, not the 1984 Bruce Springsteen song of the same name.

The evidence

Wikipedia states the song, with music by Arthur Schwartz and lyrics by Howard Dietz, was published by Harms in 1931 and introduced in the 1931 Broadway revue The Band Wagon. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1931, that is 2027.

Recordings are a different right

The song being free does not make every recording free. A recording has its own, separate copyright on its own clock. If you need the song for a video or a new release, perform or record it yourself.

Where people know it from

It is best known from the 1953 film The Band Wagon, danced by Fred Astaire and Cyd Charisse (source).
