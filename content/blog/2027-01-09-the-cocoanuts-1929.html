Is The Cocoanuts public domain? Yes, and unusually cleanly: the film, its recorded soundtrack and the Broadway show it came from are all free in the US.

Can you use The Cocoanuts for free?

The Cocoanuts was published by Paramount in 1929. Its 95-year US copyright term, as extended by the 1998 Sonny Bono Act, expired on 1 January 2025, and Duke Law's Center for the Study of the Public Domain lists it as a Public Domain Day 2025 title. We searched the 1956-1958 renewal-era Catalog of Copyright Entries ourselves and could not locate a renewal entry, though OCR gaps in those scanned volumes mean we cannot say for certain it was never renewed. It does not change the outcome either way: every public-domain-day source dates this film's US expiry to 2025, consistent with the full 95-year term having simply run out.

Does The Cocoanuts's score count as public domain too?

The soundtrack is free for the same reason as the print — it is embedded in the 1929 release and shares its term. The underlying Irving Berlin songs, written for the 1925 Broadway production, are independently free too: works from 1925 entered the US public domain no later than 1 January 2021. The story is free on the same basis: Morrie Ryskind adapted the film from the 1925 Broadway musical "The Cocoanuts," book by George S. Kaufman, music and lyrics by Berlin, and that stage work's term has also expired.

The one layer that is not free is Universal's 2016 restoration, released as part of "The Marx Brothers Silver Screen Collection" Blu-ray. A faithful restoration adds no new copyright to the underlying 1929 images, but any new material Universal added — remastered audio, new titles — can be separately protected, and ripping the copy-protected disc risks a DMCA violation regardless of the film's public-domain status. Trademark status was not checked. Commercial use that leans on the Marx Brothers' likenesses may also run into state-law publicity rights the active Marx estate still enforces, separate from copyright.

What's the history behind The Cocoanuts?

Directed by Robert Florey and Joseph Santley, The Cocoanuts was the Marx Brothers' first feature film, built around Groucho running a failing Florida resort hotel during the 1920s land boom while a jewel-theft plot unfolds around him.
