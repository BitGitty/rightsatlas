Is "Shine On, Harvest Moon" public domain? Yes. The 1908 song has been free in the US since 1 January 2004 at the latest. Anyone can now perform it, arrange it, or print the sheet music without permission.

The evidence

The song is credited to Nora Bayes (music) and Jack Norworth (lyrics). Wikipedia states it debuted in the Ziegfeld Follies of 1908, with sheet music dated the same year. A library catalog record at York University Libraries lists the original sheet music as published by Jerome H. Remick & Co. in 1908, crediting the same two writers, so two sources agree on the year. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1908, that is 2004, so the song has now been free to use for more than two decades.

Recordings are a different right

A free song is not a free performance. See our explainer on why a song and a recording are different copyrights. Any specific recording of this song, including a filmed performance, has its own separate copyright on its own clock.

Where people know it from

Laurel and Hardy performed a song-and-dance routine to it in the 1939 RKO film "The Flying Deuces" (source).
