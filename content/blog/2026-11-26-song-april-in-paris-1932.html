Is "April in Paris" public domain? Not yet. The song is still under US copyright, and it becomes free on 1 January 2028 at the latest. Here is the evidence behind that date.

The short answer

The music is by Vernon Duke and the lyrics are by E. Y. Harburg. Wikipedia states the song was written in 1932 for the Broadway musical "Walk a Little Faster," and published that year by Harms. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1932, that is 2028. From that date, anyone will be able to perform the song, print new sheet music, or release a fresh recording of it without asking permission.

Recordings are a different right

Even once the song itself is free, a recording of it is not. A sound recording has its own copyright, with its own clock, separate from the song. Count Basie's famous 1955 recording stays protected on its own schedule regardless of what happens to the underlying 1932 composition. The safe path for a free performance is always to record or perform the song yourself, not to lift someone else's recording.

Where people know it from

The best-known recorded version is Count Basie's 1955 instrumental, which was inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame (source).
