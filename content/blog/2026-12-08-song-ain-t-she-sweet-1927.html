Is "Ain't She Sweet" public domain? Yes. The 1927 song has been free in the US since 1 January 2023 at the latest. Anyone can now perform it, arrange it, or print the sheet music without asking permission.

The evidence

The music is by Milton Ager and the lyrics are by Jack Yellen. Wikipedia states it was published in 1927 by Ager, Yellen & Bornstein, and IMSLP's catalog lists the same 1927 edition, so we have two sources agreeing on the year. A song published in year Y is free in the US from 1 January of Y + 96 at the latest. For 1927, that is 2023.

Recordings are a different right

The song being free does not free every recording of it. See our explainer on why a song and a recording are different copyrights: a sound recording has its own, separate clock. The Beatles' well-known 1961 recording with Tony Sheridan, released as a US single in 1964, is still a protected recording in its own right, even though the 1927 song underneath it is not.

Where people know it from

Many people know it today through The Beatles' recording with Tony Sheridan, which became a US single in 1964 (source).
