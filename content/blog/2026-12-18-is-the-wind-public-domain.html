Why the film print is free

The Wind (1928). Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer released the film in 1928. In the US, every work published in 1930 or earlier is now public domain by term expiry, since the maximum 95-year copyright has run out. A 1928 release clears that bar with no renewal question needed. For most 1928 films the old 28-year renewal, which for this title would have fallen due around 1955 to 1957, is the route researchers check first. Term expiry makes that search unnecessary here: whether or not MGM ever renewed, the 95-year clock has already run out on its own.

A little history

According to Wikipedia, Victor Sjöström directed The Wind for MGM, with Lillian Gish, Lars Hanson and Montagu Love in the lead roles, from a Frances Marion adaptation of Dorothy Scarborough's novel. American critics dismissed it on its November 1928 release, but it is now ranked among the great silent films, and the US Library of Congress selected it for the National Film Registry in 1993.
