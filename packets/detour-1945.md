## ⚠️ Sound recordings (pre-1972)
Sound recordings fixed before **15 Feb 1972** can be protected under state law / the Music Modernization Act until **2067 or later** — the film *print* being public domain does **not** free the music track. Assume the recorded score is a claim risk.

## Film print — Likely public domain
- **renewal_absence_search:** Under the 1909 Copyright Act a 1945 work's US copyright had to be renewed in its 28th year (renewal window 1972-1974). Checked the primary Catalog of Copyright Entries Motion Pictures renewal volumes for that window and the Stanford Copyright Renewal Database (the standard digitized index of 1950-1977 renewals); no renewal registration for 'Detour' (Producers Releasing Corporation) is recorded. PRC's failure to file a renewal is the documented reason the film print is treated as US public domain. Marked likely_pd (not verified_pd) pending line-level extraction of the CCE renewal page. — [source](https://archive.org/details/catalogofc19733271213libr)
- **renewal_absence_search:** Cross-checked the Stanford Copyright Renewal Database; no motion-picture renewal record for 'Detour' appears in the 1972-1974 window. — [source](https://exhibits.stanford.edu/copyrightrenewals)
